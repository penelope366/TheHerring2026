Conduct and Ethics

At The Herring, we report the truth and strive to deliver the news honestly and fairly. All members uphold the journalistic integrity of The Herring and preserve a high standard of writing.

We do not allow deception, fabrication, plagiarism or theft. We make a point of avoiding bias and reducing conflicts of interest to a minimum. For corrections and clarifications, please contact auctheherring@gmail.com.

The Herring does not use AI to generate or edit work.

The Herring follows clear interview guidelines to protect journalists and sources. Every interviewee has to agree to the following conditions prior to an interview. Everything that has been said is on the record unless clearly stated beforehand. The Herring refrains from sharing complete drafts with interviewees. Quotes can and will be shared with sources for correctness. Quotes may be redacted, however they cannot be modified. Their phrasing may be clarified upon request to avoid misrepresentation.
